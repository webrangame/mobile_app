## Full Description

🛒 **Stop overpaying at the supermarket. DealMate finds you the best price — instantly.**

DealMate is Australia's smartest AI-powered grocery price comparison app. Just ask it anything — *"How much is Connoisseur Ice Cream at Woolworths?"* — and get real-time prices, side-by-side comparisons, and the best deal in seconds.

Whether you're comparing **Coles vs Woolworths**, hunting for weekly specials, or looking for the cheapest product in NSW, DealMate does the research for you.

---

### 🔍 What Can DealMate Do?
- **AI Price Comparison** — Ask in plain English, get instant answers with prices across multiple stores
- **Voice & Text Search** — Search any product by typing or speaking
- **Real Product Cards** — See product images, prices, and deal badges side by side
- **Filter & Sort** — Sort by cheapest first, or filter by Coles or Woolworths
- **Store Catalogue** — Browse Coles and Woolworths catalogues directly in the app
- **Community Reviews** — Rate your experience and read what other shoppers say
